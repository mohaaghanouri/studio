Eight hours of report writing become three. The assessment stays yours.

A Bausachverständiger doing Bauschäden and Beweissicherung, one specialising in Verkehrswertgutachten, and one who does mostly Wohnungsübergaben and small defect reports. All three are personally liable for every sentence they sign, and that single fact determines the entire shape of what we are allowed to build.

The site work takes three hours and the writing takes eight. Photos have to be numbered, placed and cross-referenced. Defects have to be described in the standard wording. Norms have to be cited exactly. The structure has to be the same every time, because a court expects it to be. And every line has to hold up under cross-examination, so it is written slowly, at night, twice.

What we build

The site capture app
Photograph, speak, move on. Each photo carries its number, room, time and spoken note. The site visit no longer produces a pile of unlabelled images that has to be reconstructed from memory two days later. That is where most of the eight hours actually goes, and where most errors creep in.
The defect entries
Dictated notes become structured entries in your own standard wording, drawn from your own library, with the correct photo references already attached. You read and correct every one.
The wording library
Your tested wordings, sorted by defect type. Not generated language: your language, which has already survived scrutiny. This distinction is what makes the whole thing acceptable. The tool retrieves proven text; it does not write new text.
The norm lookup
The exact text with its citation. Never a paraphrase, because a paraphrased norm in a Gutachten is a gift to opposing counsel.
The comparables list
For valuations: comparables assembled, sourced and dated, with the arithmetic shown step by step. The choice of method and the adjustments remain yours.

Where it stops

Raw stays raw, and the verdict is always yours.

This is the hardest stop in the entire practice and the least negotiable, because you sign and you are liable, potentially in court, personally. Cause of damage: never proposed. The system records the observation and stops. Valuation: never produced. It gathers the inputs and performs the arithmetic you specified. Severity or classification: never assigned. Every sentence in the final document was either written by you or taken word for word from your own approved library and then confirmed by you.

One further rule surprises people: the system keeps a record of what was captured when, and the raw material stays unaltered. If a report is challenged you must be able to show the original photograph, the original timestamp and the original spoken note. Digitally improved photographs, tidied notes and reconstructed timestamps would all be catastrophic.

How you see it working

Hours per report: eight down to about three, all of them now spent on assessment rather than layout. Days from site visit to delivered report. Photo referencing errors: zero. Reports per month at unchanged quality.

When we say no

Any request to draft the assessment section, however carefully hedged. Also any request to work from another Gutachter’s wordings. The library must be your own, or the wording carries no authority when it is tested.

What it asks of you

Talking on site rather than writing at night, and reading and confirming every entry before it goes into the report.
