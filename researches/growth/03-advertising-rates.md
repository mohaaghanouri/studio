# Paid advertising: real German rates and whether it's worth it

Research run 2026-08-08. All figures cited; keyword-level CPC bands are estimates
anchored to published benchmarks (no public source publishes per-keyword German CPC —
validate free in Google Keyword Planner before committing budget).

## Bottom line

For a solo operator at €500–2,000/month selling €1,000–10,000 engagements to German
Kammer-regulated professionals, **paid search and social are the wrong primary tool.**
The arithmetic doesn't close: German B2B-consulting CPCs plus realistic high-touch
conversion rates put CAC at 40–80% of a €2,000 engagement.

Two paid channels **do** work at this budget — German trade-press lead-gen products
(sponsored webinars, newsletter text ads, classified Fortbildung ads) and a deliberately
tiny long-tail Google/Bing account — because they buy access to a verified professional
list rather than an auction against agencies. Everything else subsidises Google and
LinkedIn.

## 1. Google Ads Germany

**Verified 2026 baseline**

- Cross-industry German search CPC: **~€1.30 average, €0.80–4.50 typical**, up ~18%
  since Q1 2024 as AI Overviews push more advertisers into paid
  ([next-levels](https://next-levels.de/blog/was-kosten-google-ads-budget-rechner-und-realistische-klickpreise-2026)).
- **Coaching/Weiterbildung DACH: CPC €2.90 (€1.50–5.50), CTR 5.5%, CVR 5.0%, CPL €60
  (€32–110)** — from real DACH accounts managing >€1M spend
  ([visnakovs Q3 2026](https://visnakovs.de/tools/google-ads-benchmarks)).
- **B2B/Software: CPC €4.20 (€2.20–8.50), CPL €120 (€65–220)**. IT services corroborated
  at €2.50–8.00 and €3.00–10.00 ([rankpilot](https://rankpilot.de/blog/google-ads-kosten)).
- **Rechtsanwälte CPC €5.50 (€3.00–12.00); Zahnmedizin €3.20 (€1.80–6.00)**.
- Berlin/München/Hamburg carry a **15–30% CPC premium** over rural Germany.

**Term-by-term reality**

| Term | Est. CPC | Who owns the auction |
|---|---|---|
| `KI Beratung` | €6–14 | **Agency bloodbath**, plus ~90% junk intent (students, jobseekers). Avoid. |
| `KI Berater` | €5–12 | Same, plus recruiting traffic. Avoid. |
| `KI Workshop` | €3–7 | Bildungsträger, IHK, Haufe, agencies. Marginal. |
| `KI Schulung` | €3–6 | Bildungsgutschein/AZAV providers bid hard. Marginal. |
| `ChatGPT Schulung Unternehmen` | €4–9 | Commercial intent, thin volume, agency-heavy. Will starve on volume. |
| `KI Schulung Steuerberater` / `KI Kanzlei` / `KI Zahnarztpraxis` | **€1.50–4** | **Thin or empty auctions. The only place a solo wins.** |

Context on the agency terms: on "SEO Agentur München" the Google-vs-Bing CPC delta alone
was **€10.13/click** ([webfeinschliff](https://www.webfeinschliff.de/ratgeber/microsoft-ads/)),
implying €12+ Google CPCs on agency-service terms.

**Minimum viable test:** floor **€500/month**; below €300 you never leave the noise floor.
Exact + phrase match only, Search only (kill Display and Search Partners day one),
manual bidding until 30 conversions, aggressive negatives (`Ausbildung`, `Studium`,
`Jobs`, `Gehalt`, `kostenlos`, `Zertifikat`, `IHK`, `Bildungsgutschein`, `Praktikum`,
`Definition`). **Read after €1,500–2,000 cumulative spend**, not after one month.
Realistic CPL €60–200; treat above €150 as a fail.

## 2. LinkedIn Ads — the numbers that end the conversation

- DACH averages: **CPC €4–8, CPM ~€30, CTR 0.4–0.8%, CPL €10–400**
  ([mmmake](https://mmmake.com/de/blog/linkedin-ads-benchmarks-dach-2025/)); 2024 actuals
  CPC €5.75, CPM €31 ([zweidigital](https://www.zweidigital.de/en/linkedin-cpm-cpc-ctr-benchmarks/)).
- **Management Consulting DACH 2026: CPC €9–18, CPM €100–200, CPL from €150**
  ([saupe](https://www.saupe-communication.de/post/linkedin-ads-kosten-benchmark-2026)).
- Minimum practical budget **€50–100/day = €1,500–5,000/month**.

**Verdict: a money pit below ~€3,000/month, and the ticket size never justifies it.**
Worse, the best segments barely exist there — practising dentists, small-town
Steuerberater and therapists are structurally underrepresented, and targeting them by
job title yields audiences so small LinkedIn's over-narrow-targeting penalty makes CPCs
"shoot through the roof". Only SME executives and marketers are properly reachable, and
those are the least differentiated segments.

Format notes: **Lead Gen Forms** beat landing pages on CPL but harvest low-intent leads —
bad trade for a €2,000 high-touch sale. **Thought Leader Ads** (boosting a personal post)
clear **>1% CTR vs 0.4–0.8%** and are the only format worth a solo's money.
**Message/InMail:** reputational poison for this buyer set in Germany.

**The real exception:** use LinkedIn's *free* side — Sales Navigator or plain search — to
build lists and send connection requests with a personal note. Same targeting data,
€0–100/month, and in Germany it's the compliant substitute for cold email (§5).

## 3. Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Germany

- Germany 2026: **CPM €5–15, CPC €0.30–2.50**. Bildung/Coaching CPC €0.60–1.50;
  B2B/SaaS CPC €1.00–2.50 ([ostend.digital](https://ostend.digital/facebook-ads-kosten/)).
  Germany sits ~28% below the global CPM benchmark.
- German B2B CPL for explanation-heavy services: **€40–120**.
- International professional-services benchmark **$67.50 CPL**, up 23% YoY; native lead
  forms run 32% lower CPL ([get-ryze](https://www.get-ryze.ai/blog/meta-ads-cost-lead-generation-b2b-benchmarks)).

Clicks are 5–15× cheaper than LinkedIn and the same decision-makers are there — but you
cannot buy *intent*, and a €2,000 engagement requires intent. Apply a cold-social close
rate of 3–8% and CPLs of €40–120 become **CAC €500–4,000**.

**Where Meta genuinely wins:** filling a €49–299 webinar or course, and **retargeting**
site visitors. A €100–150/month retargeting-only campaign at €5–15 CPM is one of the
highest-ROI line items available. As cold acquisition for €1k–10k engagements: no.

## 4. Other channels

**Google Local Services Ads — not available to you.** LSA is live in Germany but the
supported category list does not include business consulting, coaching, training or IT
consulting ([Google](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/6224841?hl=de&co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DGermany)).
The pay-per-lead model would have suited perfectly. Dead end — stop researching it.

**Microsoft/Bing Ads — cheap, tiny, demographically perfect.** German share 9.7% overall
but **15–18% desktop**, CPCs **30–50% below Google**, audience older/more affluent/B2B on
Microsoft 365 during work hours. **Edge is the default browser on every Kanzlei and
Steuerbüro Windows desktop in Germany.** Reality check: 15% desktop share of a long tail
with ~30 monthly searches = 2–5 clicks/month. **A €50–100/month always-on line item, not
a channel.** Import the Google campaigns and forget it. Best cost-per-euro-of-effort in
the paid stack.

**YouTube cold pre-roll:** no citable 2026 German CPV found, so no estimate given. Skip —
your buyer's problem is trust, not awareness. If you have video, spend it on retargeting.

### German trade press — where the money should actually go

Real 2026 rate cards. Note the price per decision-maker.

**zm Zahnärztliche Mitteilungen** — Auflage **78,000** ≈ every German dentist
([Mediadaten 2026 PDF](https://www.zm-online.de/fileadmin/user_upload/zm/mediadaten/MTX_ZM_Mediadaten_2026_web_3.pdf)):
- 1/1 page 4c €8,700 — forget it.
- **Rubrikenanzeige (incl. Fortbildung): €7.30/mm 4c.** A 50mm single-column ad ≈
  **€350–365 to reach 78,000 dentists = ~€4.70 effective CPM.** Against LinkedIn
  Management Consulting CPM of €100–200, that is **20–40× cheaper reach into a
  better-qualified audience.**
- Online TKP €75–155. Newsletter Teaser €3,120. Exclusive standalone newsletter to
  19,500 dentists €5,900 (18–25% open). *zm starter* standalone to 5,000 younger
  dentists €1,950.

**Anwaltsblatt (DAV)** — tvA **58,318**
([Mediadaten 2026 PDF](https://anwaltsblatt.anwaltverein.de/files/anwaltsblatt.de/downloads/mediadaten/anwaltsblatt-mediadaten-2026.pdf)):
- 1/1 page €4,690–6,190; 1/16 page €470–620.
- **Newsletter: 44,000 recipients, Ø 40% open. Top slot €2,050.**
- **Online-Advertorial €2,450** — article-style page on anwaltsblatt.de, homepage teasers
  for 2 weeks, one placement in the 44,000-recipient newsletter, **indexed on the domain
  for at least a year.** The best-constructed product on this list.

**Rheinisches Ärzteblatt** (ÄK Nordrhein + KV, Auflage 55,425)
([PDF](https://www.aekno.de/fileadmin/user_upload/aekno/downloads/2025/media-wwf-2026.pdf)):
1/1 page 4c €5,654; 1/6 page €1,071; **Rubrikenanzeige €5.20–7.98/mm** with a dedicated
"Fortbildung/Veranstaltungen" rubric. Repeat discounts to 20% at 24 insertions. Every
regional Ärztekammer has an equivalent — a cheap, repeatable city-by-city play.

**LOHN+GEHALT (DATAKONTEXT)** — small print run (6,200) but the digital products are the
point ([Mediadaten 2026 PDF](https://www.lohnundgehalt-magazin.de/app/uploads/2025/12/LUG_Mediadaten_2026.pdf)):
- **Newsletter, 8,000+ recipients** (HR/payroll leads, **Steuerberater und Kanzleien**),
  fortnightly: **Textanzeige €500, Premium €700.** Standalone Sponsored News €2,950.
- **★ Sponsored Webinar as sole sponsor: from €2,650.** 45 min talk + 15 min Q&A,
  **GDPR-compliant attendee contact list with Werbeeinwilligung**, attendance record,
  submitted questions, survey results, recording for your own sales use, promoted to
  8,500+ contacts.
- HR & Payroll Tools Days 2026 (Cologne): Basic sponsor with 30-min pitch **€3,450**.
- 2026 editorial calendar is littered with the topic — issues on "Digitalisierung/KI",
  "Payroll-KI", a webconference "KI – Tools und Lösungen für HR". **Pitch the editors
  before you pay anyone.**

**NWB "IK"** — tvA only 1,159, but every reader is a Kanzlei decision-maker. 1/1 page 4c
€1,950; 1/4 page €792.

**Do these reach decision-makers better than platforms? Yes, decisively** — Kammer
publications arrive by post to every licensed member, a precision no ad platform can
match, at a fraction of the CPM. **The catch: a lump-sum bet with no in-flight
optimisation and no A/B test. Budget two insertions minimum or don't start.**

## 5. Ranked for €500–2,000/month

**Do these**

1. **Trade-press sponsored webinar (from €2,650) — highest expected return of anything
   paid.** 45 minutes teaching your exact buyer, plus a consented contact list of
   everyone who attended, plus a recording. At 40–80 attendees that's **€33–66 per warm
   lead who watched you work for 45 minutes.** Nothing on Google or LinkedIn comes within
   5× that quality-per-euro.
2. **Trade-press newsletter text ad, €500–700** to 8,000 Steuerberater/HR decision-makers.
   Cheapest real test in this report.
3. **Kammer-press classified Fortbildung ads, €350–600 each.** Absurd reach-per-euro. Run
   monthly for the 20% Malstaffel discount.
4. **Google Ads, long-tail profession-specific only, €400–600/month.** Not `KI Beratung`.
5. **Microsoft Ads mirror, €50–100/month.** Zero incremental effort.
6. **Meta retargeting only, €100–150/month.**
7. **Anwaltsblatt Online-Advertorial €2,450** — once you have one strong lawyer-specific piece.

**Avoid outright:** LinkedIn paid · broad `KI Beratung`/`KI Berater` terms · Meta cold
prospecting · YouTube cold pre-roll · Google LSA (category unsupported) · full-page print
(€5,654–8,700 for one unmeasurable impression) · **any agency retainer** (a €800–1,500/mo
management fee on €500–2,000 spend means paying more to manage than to advertise).

### Non-paid, and better than all of the above at this budget

**Speaking at Kammer/Verband Fortbildung — highest ROI available, and the ROI is negative
CAC.** Every target profession has statutory CPD: Fachanwälte need 15 hours/year under
**§15 FAO**; Steuerberater have a Fortbildungspflicht under the **BOStB**; Zahnärzte and
Ärzte have Fortbildungspunkte via **§95d SGB V**. Every Kammer and Verband runs a seminar
programme, needs "KI in der Praxis" urgently, has no idea who to ask, and **pays a
speaker honorarium.** You get paid to stand in front of 30–80 pre-qualified buyers who
are legally obliged to be in the room. **Send five pitches to Kammer Fortbildungsreferate
before spending one euro on ads.**

**Editorial before advertorial.** A contributed article costs €0 and outperforms the
€2,450 advertorial on trust. Editors' contacts are in the same media kits.

**Referral and partnership.** DATEV Beratungs-/Systempartner, Kanzlei-software resellers,
Praxisberater, Kanzlei-IT houses, IHK/HWK digital advisors, coworking and Gründerzentren.
Zero CAC, and referred-lead close rates are multiples of cold paid.

**Direct outreach — with the German legal constraint stated plainly.** Public registries
exist (BRAK, KZBV Zahnarztsuche, DStV). **But unsolicited commercial email to businesses
without prior consent is unlawful under § 7 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 UWG — there is no B2B carve-out.**
Cold phone calls to businesses need at least *mutmaßliche Einwilligung* (§ 7 Abs. 2 Nr. 1)
— defensible but thin. **Clean: postal mail, LinkedIn connection requests with a personal
note, in-person contact at Kammer events.** A 200-piece personalised letter to Berlin
Kanzleien costs ~€200–300 and lands on a desk where nothing else does. Verify with a
lawyer before scaling — you're selling *to* lawyers.

## 6. Unit economics

**Sustainable CAC on a €2,000 engagement**

| | One-off | With expansion |
|---|---|---|
| First engagement | €2,000 | €2,000 |
| Expansion multiple | 1.0× | **1.8–2.5×** |
| Effective LTV | €2,000 | **€3,600–5,000** |
| Referrals (0.3–0.5 typical, high-touch) | — | +€600–2,500 |
| **Sustainable CAC (20% of LTV)** | **€400** | **€720–1,000** |
| Absolute ceiling (30%) | €600 | €1,100–1,500 |

**Plan against €400–600 CAC.** Use €1,000 only once repeat-purchase data exists. Above
€1,500 you work for the ad platform.

**Realistic funnel, cold paid traffic, €2,000 high-touch sale**

| Stage | Pessimistic | Realistic | Good |
|---|---|---|---|
| Click → enquiry | 1.0% | **2.0%** | 4.0% |
| Enquiry → held call | 40% | **55%** | 70% |
| Call → paid client | 10% | **20%** | 30% |
| **Click → client** | 0.04% | **0.22%** | 0.84% |
| **Clicks per client** | 2,500 | **455** | 119 |

Baselines: visitor→lead 1.5–2.5% average, 3–5% strong; lead→meeting 12–18%;
opportunity→customer 31–39% ([delverise](https://delverise.com/blog/b2b-sales-funnel-conversion-rates-benchmarks-2026),
[lunas](https://www.lunas.consulting/post/real-b2b-sales-conversion-rate-benchmarks-and-what-high-performing-teams-achieve-in-2026)).

**Applying CPC**

| Channel | CPC | CAC (realistic) | CAC (good) | vs €400–600 target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google long-tail | €2.50 | **€1,138** | €298 | Only at "good" |
| Microsoft Ads | €1.75 | **€796** | €208 | Best paid-search economics |
| Google broad | €9.00 | €4,095 | €1,071 | **Never** |
| LinkedIn (Mgmt Consulting) | €13.50 | €6,143 | €1,607 | **Never** |
| Meta cold | €1.20 | €1,100+ after intent discount | | No |
| **Trade-press webinar** | €2,650 / ~60 attendees @ 15% | **≈€294** | | **✓** |
| **Kammer speaking** | negative (paid honorarium) | | | **✓✓** |

**Three free things decide whether paid search works at all:**

1. **Click→enquiry ≥3%** — needs a landing page per profession
   (`/ki-fuer-steuerberater`, `/ki-zahnarztpraxis`), not a generic homepage.
2. **A low-friction first step** — "Kostenloses 20-Minuten-Erstgespräch" with direct
   calendar booking, not a contact form. Worth more than doubling the budget.
3. **Reply within 60 minutes** during business hours. Largest single lever on
   lead→meeting, and free.

**Also publish a price anchor.** "Workshops ab €1.900" or "Coaching ab €1.200". German
professional buyers self-disqualify silently when there's no price, and you pay for their
click either way.

## The capacity argument nobody makes

At €2,000/engagement and high-touch delivery you can serve **3–5 new clients/month** solo.
So you need 3–5 clients/month, not 50 — and that is achievable through Kammer speaking,
referral and two trade-press placements per quarter, with **zero platform ad spend.**
**Paid acquisition solves a volume problem you do not have, at a CAC your ticket cannot
carry.**
