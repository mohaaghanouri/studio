# Monetisation for a solo AI consultant in Berlin

Research run 2026-08-08. Two classes of data are kept separate throughout:

- **Hard numbers** — contractual, statutory or association-published: the BDVT fee
  recommendation 2026, the Berlin VHS tariff, the INQA/uWM/BAFA/Coaching-BONUS day-rate
  caps, the AVGS table, published SaaS price lists. **Trust these.**
- **Asking prices** — what consultants publish, not what they collect. Treat as ceilings a
  solo operator with no testimonials won't reach in month one.

## 1. The authoritative floor: BDVT Honorarempfehlung 2026

The BDVT publishes annual **minimum** recommendations — the document a client's HR person
finds when checking whether you're expensive. Tiers: *Starter*, *Professional* (completed
training, references, own brand, several years), *Senior-Professional*. **Six years and
real shipped systems puts him at Professional, arguably its top.**

| Service (net, BDVT 2026) | Starter | Professional | Senior-Prof. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Training, no own acquisition/design (day) | from €950 | from €1,200 | — |
| Training, standard content / large volume (day) | from €1,200 | from €1,500 | from €2,600 |
| Training, custom content / certificate / proven impact (day) | from €1,500 | **from €2,000** | from €3,300 |
| **Coaching, per hour** | from €200 | **from €275** | from €440 |
| Consulting (day) | from €1,500 | **from €2,000** | from €3,300 |
| Design/prep/follow-up (day) | from €1,500 | from €2,000 | from €3,300 |
| Online training, standard, **per 90 min** | from €400 | from €460 | from €860 |
| Online training, custom, **per 90 min** | from €500 | **from €660** | from €1,100 |
| Webinar ≤20 participants (~90 min) | from €800 | | |
| Webinar 21+ (with chat moderator) | from €1,740 | | |

Commercially important rules:
- **"3 sessions × 90 min = 1 day rate"** — the official online conversion. Use it; it stops
  clients buying a day's content at a 90-minute price.
- **Recordings with usage rights: charge factor 10.** Almost nobody enforces this. Highest-
  leverage line in the document.
- VAT, travel, overnight, **travel time**, room, catering and documentation are all billed
  **on top**.
- BDVT names public education providers ("often under €500/day") as pushing freelancers into
  poverty, but concedes subcontracting through institutes legitimately pays below
  recommendation because they carry acquisition.
([BDVT PDF](https://www.bdvt.de/bdvt-wAssets/docs/Berufsstaendische-Informationen/Honorarempfehlung.pdf))

**Market context:** German freelance average **~€101.70/h**, consulting/management
**~€121/h**; IT freelance day rates **€600–900**; BDU average *billed* consultancy day
**€1,000** (partner €1,600, analyst €700). German business coaching: market average
**€165–180/h**, experienced €150–300/h, **private clients €120–330/h vs corporate
€220–700/h** — the same hour is worth ~2× when a company pays. German keynote
**€3,000–15,000**. IHK honorary lecturers from **€950/day**. **Berlin VHS: €45.31 per
45-min unit** from 1 Aug 2026 ≈ €270/day.

**German AI-specific asking prices** (anchors to price *against*): half-day workshop from
**€1,580**; full day **€1,900–2,480**; 2-day intensive **€2,800–4,480**; executive briefing
**€2,480**; online workshop **€1,200–1,900**; pre-workshop process analysis **€980**; extra
participants **€120–180** each; recording **€350**; follow-up coaching **€600/mo**; annual
4-workshop package from **€8,000**; AI readiness assessment **€990–1,990**; strategy
roadmap fixed **€2,000–3,500**; implementation from **€4,990–8,000** (range €2,490–29,990);
solo/freelance AI day rate **€800–1,500**.

Retail per-seat cross-check in his niches: **NWB Akademie AI course for tax firms €345/person**;
**Digitale Kanzlei Art.-4 training €99 net/person**.

## 2. The arbitrage: DACH vs US for identical work

| | DACH (realistic) | US (published) |
|---|---|---|
| Hourly | €150–275 | **$150–350**, senior $300–500 |
| Day rate | €800–1,500 | $600–1,200 freelance, **$1,500–3,000** top |
| Half-day team workshop | **€1,580–2,500** | **$3,000–8,000** |
| Full-day workshop | €1,900–4,500 | **$6,000–15,000** |
| Monthly retainer, solo | €1,500–4,000 | **$2,000–10,000**; fractional CAIO $5,000–30,000 |
| Diagnostic / audit | €990–2,900 | **$1,500–3,000** productized |
| Keynote | €3,000–15,000 | $10,000–50,000 |

France as a third data point: **BPI France's Diag Data IA values 8 consultant-days at
€10,000** with a 25% subsidy from Jan 2026 — i.e. €1,250/day is the French state's view of
a fair AI consultant day rate ([Tensoria](https://tensoria.fr/en/blog/ai-audit-method-cost)).

**The arbitrage is the finding.** Identical prep, identical Zoom room: a half-day workshop
is €1,600–2,500 in Germany and $3,000–8,000 in the US; retainers are 2–3×. **His
English-language site is therefore not decoration** — remote English workshops and retainers
for US/UK/CH buyers are the fastest available price increase requiring no new skill. Swiss
buyers are the nearest version (CHF rates 20–40% above German). *Caveat:* US buyers expect a
track record he doesn't yet have, and payment/withholding friction is real. **Month-4 move,
not month-1.**

## 3. Revenue models ranked for a solo operator

Ranked by (effective hourly) × (probability of starting inside 90 days). Effective hourly
includes prep, notes, admin and the unpaid sales call.

### Tier 1 — now

1. **Inhouse team workshop, single profession.** €2,200 half-day / €3,400 full day net.
   6–8h prep the *first* time, 2h every time after, because the content is identical for the
   40th dental practice. **Effective ~€220/h first run, ~€360/h on repeats.** This is the
   real productisation — repeatable content, not software. Caps around €150–200k/year at
   full utilisation, which is a fine ceiling. **Highest-ROI model available, full stop.**
2. **Paid diagnostic/audit as tripwire.** €1,200 net fixed, one week, written deliverable.
   ~€150/h — *deliberately mediocre*, because its job is qualification, testimonials and
   conversion, not margin.
3. **Fixed-scope implementation project.** €4,000–12,000, 50% deposit, single named
   deliverable, hard change-order boundary. €150–300/h if scoped tightly, catastrophically
   negative if not. He's already doing this, just unpriced.
4. **Monthly retainer / fractional advisory.** €1,500/mo (½ day + async) or €2,900/mo
   (1 day + async), 3-month minimum, invoiced in advance. **~€290/h — best in the list.**
   Sold to clients who already trust you, essentially never cold. **Where the business
   should end up, not where it starts.**

### Tier 2 — needs a precondition

5. **1:1 coaching sold as a package.** €250/h single; 6-session package €1,350 (~€225/session).
   ~€130/h after notes. The fix isn't the price — it's **selling six sessions at once**,
   which triples cash per sales conversation and kills rebooking churn. Note the spread: the
   same six sessions billed to a dental practice support €400–500/h; billed to a PhD student,
   €120–150/h. **The PhD-student and job-seeker segments cannot pay professional rates and
   should be served by a product, not his calendar.**
6. **Cohort course.** 8–12 seats × €800–1,500 = €6,400–18,000 per run. Cohorts complete at
   85–96% vs 10–20% self-paced and command 2–5× the price. Critically, **a cohort can be
   pre-sold before it exists** and fills from a list of 200–300. Month 3–4. **The correct
   first digital product.**
7. **Speaking.** €1,500 starting in DACH, €2,500 with three references. Honest: with no reel
   he'll be offered €0–1,500 and "exposure". **Treat paid talks as revenue and unpaid talks
   as workshop lead-gen, capped at one a month, only where the room is his ICP.**

### Tier 3 — not yet

8. **Self-paced course.** €197–497 is the German sweet spot. The arithmetic against his
   actual audience: median free-to-paid newsletter conversion **0.62%** (top quartile 2–5%);
   course funnels 1–5%. **A €297 course into a 300-person list at 3% = €2,700 gross for 100+
   hours of production**, before 25–35% platform fees. **Build it later, out of cohort
   recordings students already paid for. Never first.**
9. **Licensing his tools.** See §5. Right option, wrong sequence.
10. **Affiliate/referral.** Google cut Workspace *renewal* partner margin from 20% to 12% in
    April 2024; reseller margins concentrate in volume tiers he'll never hit. **Take fees
    from tools he already recommends, disclose in writing, never let them shape a
    recommendation. Do not model revenue on this** — the reputational downside for someone
    whose pitch is "honesty about limits" dwarfs the money.

## 4. The productisation ladder

The escape from hourly billing is **not** software. It's: *make the same thing twice, at a
fixed price, for buyers who look identical.* Two mechanisms.

### Mechanism 1: the paid diagnostic

Split the discovery you already do for free into a standalone product with fixed scope, a
repeatable framework and a written deliverable. Typical **$2,000–5,000**, functioning as a
lead-in to retainer and implementation work.

Real examples: **Brennan Dunn's roadmapping at $6,000**, where clients who bought a roadmap
then paid **~10×** for delivery. **Jonathan Stark** reached an effective **$2,000+/hour** on
roadmapping sessions — then retired the offer and turned the pattern into a book, which is
instructive: **the diagnostic is a phase, not a destination.** Reported conversion: 30–50%
audit→client on productized audits; **plan on 25–35% and be pleased at 40%.**

Why it works mechanically: a €1,200 fixed-scope audit **removes committee approval** — it
sits under most SME signature thresholds and shortens the cycle to one conversation.

**Smallest paid product that qualifies buyers: €500–1,500 in DACH.** Below ~€300 you attract
tyre-kickers and the qualification signal vanishes (the failure mode of the free first
conversation). Above ~€2,500 you reintroduce the approval process. The German market has
converged here already: readiness assessments €990–1,990, process analysis €980, fixed
roadmaps €2,000–3,500. **€1,200 net is the right number** — clears qualification, sits under
a practice owner's discretionary limit, and anchors €4,000–12,000 as "3–10× the audit"
rather than "€8,000 from zero".

### Mechanism 2: identical clients

The audit only escapes hourly billing if the *findings repeat*. Twenty dental practices
produce roughly the same twelve findings; twenty clients from eighteen professions produce
240 unique findings and he's back to bespoke consulting at €150/h forever.

**This is the binding constraint on the current site. Eighteen professions is a positioning
statement, not a niche.** Same audit, same slides, same three automations → prep collapses
8h → 2h, effective rate €150 → €360/h, and referrals compound inside a professional
community that talks to itself constantly.

**The ladder:** free 20-min triage → **€1,200 audit** → €2,200–3,400 workshop *or*
€4,000–12,000 build → **€1,500–2,900/mo retainer** → €800–1,500 cohort seat for everyone who
can't afford the ladder.

The tools are **delivery leverage inside** the €4,000–12,000 rung, not products of their own.

## 5. German-specific levers, ranked by money ÷ barrier

### Worth doing

**BAFA "Förderung unternehmerischen Know-hows" — do this before 31 December 2026.** The
programme **ends then with no successor announced**. Grant **50% in old Länder, up to 80% in
new Länder *and Berlin***; max grant €3,500 (some sources €5,600); application filed
**before** consulting begins. Requires listing in the **BAFA Beraterdatenbank** —
administrative registration, not an audited certification. **He is in Berlin, where the rate
is 80%: a €4,000 engagement costs a Berlin SME €800.** Not a subsidy — a closing tool.
~5 months of runway.

**Coaching BONUS Berlin.** 50% subsidy, eligible day rate capped **€1,000 net**, max 8 days
per project / 20 per company, **first two coaching days fully funded for a first-time
applicant**. Requires pool membership. Berlin-only, which suits him. The €1,000 cap sits
*below* the BDVT Professional floor of €2,000/day — **use it for acquisition and case
studies, never let a funded rate become your published rate.**

**unternehmensWert:Mensch (uWM) — lowest barrier of any funded programme.** Max 10 days in
9 months at **max €1,000/day net**; 50% subsidy, **80% for companies with ≤9 employees** —
precisely the size of a dental practice or small Kanzlei. **Authorisation is essentially an
information event plus an annual exchange.** Berlin Erstberatungsstelle run by f-bb.

**EU AI Act Article 4 — the strongest commercial hook in the German market right now, and
not a subsidy at all.** In force since **2 Feb 2025**; **supervisory authorities can impose
penalties from 2 August 2026.** No fixed curriculum, but competence must be **documented and
provable** — who was trained, when, on what. A per-employee certificate discharges it.
**Sober caveat to pass on honestly: Art. 4 alone carries no direct fine** — the real exposure
is liability via general duty of care. Anyone selling "€35m fine for not training staff" is
lying, and he shouldn't. **Why it's the lever:** it converts AI training from discretionary
into a **compliance line item with a deliverable**, bought by a risk-averse buyer, on a
deadline that has already passed — and his segments are the most compliance-reflexive buyers
in Germany.

**Kammer / CPD channels — cheap credibility, mediocre direct pay.** **Fachanwälte: 15
clock-hours/year per specialisation under §15 FAO**, online counts fully. **Dentists: 125
CPD points per 5 years**; a speaker earns 2 points per event, a small incentive to have you
present. Chambers deliberately don't pre-certify events, so no accreditation gate — but brief
attendees accurately. Direct honoraria are ordinary trainer rates. **The value is the room,
not the fee:** 30 Praxisinhaber who all need Art. 4 documentation, one of whom buys a €3,400
inhouse day.

**Bildungszeit Berlin — one cheap admin trick.** Application to the Senatsverwaltung **≥10
weeks before start**, didactic programme attached; **live online counts, purely asynchronous
does not.** Recognition means an employee attends **on paid work time**, converting "I'd like
to but I'm busy" into a yes.

### Defer

**INQA-Coaching.** Best-paying funded programme: **80% of fees at max €1,200/day, up to 12
days** ≈ €14,400 per authorised engagement. But the 2026 coach-pool window (16 March –
16 April) has closed, and the bar is substantive: degree/training **plus 5 years**
experience, ≥3 years consulting, competence in ≥2 of 6 INQA design fields, and **a
process-consulting qualification of ≥120 theory + ≥60 practice hours.** **He likely fails the
120/60 requirement today. Start accumulating it now; apply spring 2027.**

### Actively avoid

**AZAV / Bildungsgutschein / §82 SGB III.** The prize looks big — AZAV courses €4,000–12,000
covered up to 100%; under §82, employers with <50 staff get up to 100% of costs plus wage
subsidy. The cost: **€2,000–6,000 certification, €5,000–15,000 all-in year one**, plus
€1,500–3,500/yr surveillance and €2,000–4,000/yr QM, taking 3–6 months. **The trap: AZAV
certifies him as a *Bildungsträger*, and the Bildungsträger economy pays under €500/day**
(BDVT's own words) against Berlin VHS at ~€270/day. **He'd spend €10,000 and six months to
access the worst-paying segment of the German training market. Skip it** — subcontract to an
already-certified provider and let them keep a margin, which BDVT explicitly endorses.

Same logic on **VHS / adult education: €45.31/UE is wage labour.**

**Dead programmes — stop hearing about these.** **Digital Jetzt** expired end-2023, portal
shut March 2026. **go-digital** discontinued. Mittelstand-Digital Zentren are **free to SMEs
— competition, not a channel.**

**One sleeper, with a fatal caveat.** His job-matching tool maps onto **AVGS-funded
Bewerbungscoaching**: providers bill **€30–55 per 45-min unit** (BDKS max ~€62), so a
100-unit coaching runs **€3,000–5,500 fully state-funded.** But **AVGS requires AZAV
Trägerzulassung *and* per-measure approval**, inheriting the €10,000/6-month barrier — and
€40–55/UE is only ~€55–73/hour anyway. **The move, if he wants this channel: partner with one
existing AZAV-certified Träger and licence the tool to them.**

## 6. Turning the tools into revenue

### Job-matching tool (~70 placed)

Comparable pricing: recruiting/ATS **$15–200 per user/month**; Recruiterflow
**$99/$129/$159 per user/month with a three-seat minimum**; Manatal from $19; Bullhorn
$99–165.

**The arithmetic that kills SaaS.** At €99/seat he needs **50–100 paying seats for
€5,000–10,000 MRR** in a saturated category with entrenched incumbents, a 3–9 month sales
cycle, and buyers demanding integrations, DSGVO/AVV docs, an SLA and business-hours support.
Median zero→$1M ARR for micro-SaaS is **24 months**. Support load lands exactly in the hours
he'd otherwise bill at €360/h. **Verdict: no.**

**What works, in order:**

1. **Done-with-you placement service, private pay.** €800–1,500 per candidate, 4–6 weeks,
   with the tool as the delivery mechanism. ~70 placements is a genuinely strong proof point
   — and a €1,200 service against a state-funded AVGS alternative at €3,000–5,500 makes him
   look cheap. Starts this week.
2. **Licence to one AZAV-certified Träger or outplacement firm.** They hold the
   certification, the AVGS pipeline and the marketing; he supplies tool plus training.
   Realistic white-label terms: **€500–2,000/month or 10–30% revenue share.** One customer,
   one contract, no support desk. **The correct monetisation of this asset.**
3. **White-label to small recruiting agencies** at €300–800/month — only after (2) proves the
   tool survives someone else's workflow.
4. **A €297 self-serve product for job seekers** — structurally poor; job seekers are the
   least able to pay. Lead magnet only.

### Property-search platform

German Maklersoftware starts at **€15–17/month**; entry CRMs €39–60/user; Justimmo from
€99/month + €1 per listing. Brutally commoditised, with onOffice/Flowfact/Justimmo entrenched
and IS24 owning demand. **Do not sell this as software to agents.**

**The one good option: sell it to buyers, not agents, as done-for-you.** A **€1,500–3,000
"Immobiliensuche Berlin"** engagement for a private buyer — he runs the search, the platform
is his unfair advantage. In a market where a buyer's decision is worth €400k–800k and
Maklercourtage runs to five figures, €2,000 for a systematic search is trivially justifiable,
and the buyer has cash at exactly that moment. **His site already lists "Buying a home" as a
use case — it should have a price on it.**

### SaaS alongside consulting: don't

Solo micro-SaaS averages 45% margins and ships MVPs in 2–8 weeks — but median path to $1M
ARR is 24 months, and the ~$500–1,500 launch cost is the smallest number in the equation.
**The real cost is attention, charged in exactly the currency consulting needs.** The specific
failure mode: **consulting revenue is lumpy and SaaS support is not.** A €3,400 workshop day
cannot be moved when a licensee's parser breaks.

**The correct frame: his tools are moats, not products.** They win a €4,000–12,000
implementation against someone charging the same for slideware, and deliver it in three days
instead of ten. Worth far more than €99/month. **The only licensing to pursue in 2026 is one
deal, to one partner who already has the certification and the pipeline.**

## 7. The next 90 days

**Core diagnosis: no price, no niche, no proof — and they're the same problem.** Free first
conversation + no price list + eighteen professions means every deal is bespoke, every sale
costs an unpaid hour, and no case study compounds because no two clients look alike. **He
isn't underpaid because his rates are wrong. He's underpaid because he has no repeatable
unit of sale.**

### Move 1 (weeks 1–3): one paid entry product with a public price, and one profession

Launch a **"Praxis-Check" / "Kanzlei-Check" at €1,200 net** — fixed scope, one week,
deliverable a 6–10 page written Fahrplan naming the three highest-value automations with
time-saved estimates, plus a 90-minute walkthrough. **Keep the free conversation but cap it
at 20 minutes and rename it a triage call.** Lead with **one** profession — dental practices
or tax firms, both owner-decided, compliance-reflexive, 5–15 person businesses with money and
near-identical workflows. The other seventeen stay on the site; they stop being the headline.

**Why first:** it fixes all three problems at once. Price on the page ends free discovery.
One profession makes findings repeat. And **every audit produces a written deliverable and a
quantified result — a testimonial factory**, manufacturing proof as a by-product of paid work
rather than as a favour he has to ask for. **Six audits (€7,200) should yield two
implementations (€8,000–24,000).**

### Move 2 (weeks 3–8): the inhouse workshop, with Art. 4 as the reason to buy now

Publish two SKUs: **AI-Act Art. 4 Basisschulung, 2 hours, €1,400 net for up to 20 people**
(deliverable: per-employee certificates plus a documentation pack), and **half-day €2,200 /
full day €3,400 net**. Add "+€90 per participant above 20". **Price recordings separately at
factor 10** per BDVT — most trainers give these away.

**Why this is the highest-return move:** ~€220/h first delivery, **~€360/h on every repeat.**
Art. 4 timing is the best available in the German market and it's happening now —
enforcement began 2 August 2026, and the obligation requires documented competence with no
prescribed curriculum, which is exactly what a certificate from a credible outside trainer
provides. **The per-head maths sells itself: €1,400 for a 15-person practice is €93/head
against €345/person at NWB Akademie** — simultaneously cheaper per head and better (on-site,
their workflows). Two multipliers compound: sold to **one** buyer for **many** attendees, and
each attendee is a warm lead for a €1,350 package or the cohort.

**Guardrail:** present Art. 4 accurately. There is no direct fine for Art. 4 alone. Half the
German market is selling this with a €35m fine on the slide; given his site promises "honesty
about limits", the fear framing would cost more than the deal is worth — and "you're already
non-compliant and it's undocumented" closes anyway.

### Move 3 (weeks 2–10, in the gaps): register where registration is cheap

1. **BAFA Beraterdatenbank — highest urgency**, programme ends 31 Dec 2026, Berlin qualifies
   for **80%**, listing is administrative.
2. **uWM process-consultant pool** — information event plus annual exchange; 80% for ≤9
   employees.
3. **Coaching BONUS Berlin** — 50%, and first-time applicants get **two fully funded days**,
   a free trial he doesn't pay for.

**Why third:** these are closing tools, not lead sources — without Moves 1 and 2 there's
nothing to attach them to. But ~2–3 days of paperwork for permanent leverage, and **BAFA has
a hard deadline.**

**Hold the line on rates.** All three cap eligible day rates at €1,000–1,200, below the BDVT
Professional floor. **Charge the full rate and let the client claim the subsidy.**

**Explicitly park:** AZAV/Bildungsgutschein/AVGS · INQA until spring 2027 · VHS work ·
self-paced course · SaaS for either tool.
