## Segment top 5

### 1. Dr. Hubertus Porschen — hubertusporschen.com (DE)

The only DACH site here that would survive a comparison against good US or UK
consultant sites. The build is genuinely custom — asymmetric grid, real whitespace
discipline, no Elementor fingerprints — and the proof stack is layered rather than
dumped: enterprise logos, then mid-market logos, then associations, then press, then
a ProvenExpert score with 377 reviews behind it.

Worth stealing: the dual-CTA footer that splits "book a keynote" from "30-min
strategy call", which lets two very different buyer intents self-sort instead of
fighting over one button. Also worth stealing: the anti-hype positioning line
("KI einbauen — wie eine gute Maschine"), which speaks the customer's own vocabulary
rather than the vendor's.

### 2. Kai Spriestersbach — afaik.de (DE)

Expertise 10/10 and it is not close — four published books including a forthcoming
O'Reilly title, DFKI guest researcher, active PhD, and a blog that critiques benchmark
methodology rather than reposting release notes. The design is a plain WordPress theme
and the pricing is fully opaque, which is exactly the gap: he has more authority than
anyone else in this list and monetizes it through the weakest storefront.

The lesson runs in both directions — his content proves you can win DACH on substance
alone, and his site proves how much that substance leaves on the table. The site name
itself ("As Far As I Know") is a smart epistemic-humility flex in a market drowning
in certainty.

### 3. Lukas Görög — ki-beratung-goeroeg.com (AT/CH)

The best proof-quality in the set. While everyone else claims "measurable results",
he prints +28% dwell time, +17% app ratings, and 45 minutes saved per article, and
backs them with a verifiable prior role (AI Lead at NZZ) plus ZHAW lecturing and INMA
jury duty. The cross-border execution is quietly excellent: separate Swiss and Austrian
phone numbers, logos spanning both markets, 16 named press appearances including ORF
ZIB 2. Weakness is that the blog lives off-domain at akademie-ki.com, splitting his
authority across two properties.

### 4. Dr. Jonathan T. Mall — jonathanmall.com (DE)

The one site with actual art direction rather than "professional styling" — dark
backgrounds, cyan/amber accents, and a bespoke illustration per keynote in a different
medium (pop-art, blueprint, silkscreen). That illustration system does real work: it
makes four abstract talk topics feel like four distinct products. The neuropsychology-
plus-AI angle is a defensible niche nobody else in DACH occupies, and the Digital Twins
keynote where the audience live-interrogates synthetic personas is a genuinely
differentiated format. Broadcast logos (Tagesschau, BBC, n-tv) sit unlinked to any
article, which is the standard DACH sin.

### 5. PromptingBirds — promptingbirds.com (DE)

Best design-to-proof ratio among the boutiques: a genuinely custom, well-spaced,
color-blocked layout carrying DHL, Vorwerk, DATEV, Shiseido and GEMA logos plus
90,000+ trained employees and an AZAV certification that unlocks German public funding.
The AZAV angle is the transferable move — a certification that changes who can legally
expense you is worth more than any testimonial. Marked down on expertise because it is
a training operation: volume of people trained, not depth of AI problems solved, and no
blog to demonstrate otherwise.

## Segment-level tactic worth noting

Collective Brain, Modular Ops, Wolf gegen Licht and KBD all publish hard prices
(€390 to €14,999). In a segment where 20 of 24 sites hide behind "kostenloses
Erstgespräch", a printed number is the cheapest differentiation available.
