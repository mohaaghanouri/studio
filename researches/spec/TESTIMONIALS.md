# Testimonials — collection worklist

The site has a hidden "What clients say" / "Was Kunden sagen" section that renders the moment
quotes are added to `studio-site/src/lib/copy/en.js` and `de.js` (`testimonials.items`).
Target: 2–3 quotes, first name + profession only. German quotes weigh more for the DE page.

## Who to ask (fill in)

- [ ] A Mefkat user who landed a job
- [ ] A content creator you coached
- [ ] The house-hunt client
- [ ] Anyone from the research / exam-prep work

## Message to send (EN) — WhatsApp/Telegram tone

> Hi <name>! I'm putting my consulting work on a proper website (moha.expert). Could you do me a
> small favor? One or two honest sentences about what our work together changed for you — written
> like you'd say it, nothing polished. I'd show it as "<first name>, <profession>" — no surname,
> no link. If you'd rather not, zero problem.

## Nachricht (DE)

> Hi <Name>! Ich stelle meine Beratung gerade auf eine richtige Website (moha.expert). Magst du
> mir einen kleinen Gefallen tun? Ein, zwei ehrliche Sätze, was sich durch unsere Zusammenarbeit
> für dich geändert hat — so, wie du es sagen würdest, nichts Poliertes. Ich würde es als
> „<Vorname>, <Beruf>" zeigen — ohne Nachname, ohne Link. Wenn nicht, gar kein Problem.

## Rules

- Real quotes only, lightly trimmed for length is fine — never rewritten into marketing speak.
- Get an explicit OK for publishing with first name + profession.
- Paste raw replies to Claude; placement + translation happens then.
