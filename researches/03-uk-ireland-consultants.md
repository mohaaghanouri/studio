## Method note

WebFetch for content/structure, plus direct `curl` fingerprinting for platform, theme,
webfont stack, page weight and TTFB. Design scores therefore combine rendered layout and
IA with hard build evidence (Squarespace vs Avada vs custom Next.js, font pairing, motion
libraries, payload).

## Segment top 5

### 1. Acuity AI Advisory — acuityai.co (31/40)

The only site here that closes the loop from credibility to consequence: ex-Microsoft
Western Europe COO, a logo wall you can verify, and case studies denominated in euros and
weeks rather than percentages. The hero refuses the usual abstraction — "building what's
worth building, buying what's better bought" is a positioning statement that *eliminates*
prospects, which is exactly what it should do.

Best single move: the Copilot honesty section, which publicly reports an audit finding of
80% nominal usage vs 20% genuine engagement — attacking the category's dirty secret
rather than selling into the hype. Steal the free 3-minute "AI Fit Assessment" as a
zero-friction entry above the "Talk to us" call.

### 2. Azeem Azhar — azeemazhar.com (31/40)

Four words of hero copy — "CREATING CLARITY FROM COMPLEXITY" — carrying a page whose
entire job is done by other people's names: Daniel Ek and Accenture's CTO vouching in the
first scroll, then Bloomberg/FT/WSJ/BBC. A masterclass in letting borrowed authority do
the persuading instead of self-description. The lesson is restraint: one claim, two
testimonials from people the buyer already respects, nothing else competing. Weakness: it
monetises only keynotes — a buyer with a project has nowhere to go.

### 3. Careful Industries — careful.industries (29/40)

"Make technology work for 8 billion people, not 8 billionaires" is the only line in 24
sites with an actual point of view, and it does real qualifying work — repelling corporate
buyers and magnetising civil-society ones. The **Consequence Check** is the best
conversion asset found in this research: a free assessment tool that is genuinely useful,
demonstrates the methodology, and captures intent without a discovery call. Backed by
published research with named funders (Wellcome, JRF, Royal Academy of Engineering), so
the thought leadership is verifiable rather than blog filler. Let down by a 2.1MB
Squarespace payload.

### 4. Clearlead AI — clearlead.ai (29/40)

The most technically credible solo operator in the UK/IE set, structured to prove it
rather than assert it: PhD in IR/ML, then UnitedHealth, Samsung and Qualcomm, then three
explicitly named engagement shapes (Explore & Plan / Design & Build / Advise & Guide) that
pre-answer "how do I buy this". Its sharpest differentiator is anti-positioning against
the Big 4 — you get the senior person throughout, no junior handoff — the single most
reusable argument available to any solo consultant. Fast custom Next.js at 173KB. Gap:
every case study reports scale (30,000 products, 130 locations) and never outcome.

### 5. Spicy Advisory — spicyadvisory.com (27/40)

The only site whose typography is a deliberate decision rather than a theme default:
Anton as a condensed display face against Outfit on dark, which is why it reads as a
studio while the WordPress cohort reads as suppliers. Positioning is admirably narrow —
not "AI consulting" but *the adoption gap*, framed with a hard number (only 20% of teams
use the tools you already pay for) and a cost of inaction (~€30/month per unused licence).
Two named founders with legible pedigrees plus 4.98/5 from 12 verified reviews. Substance
is the ceiling: this is enablement and change management, not AI engineering — and the
site never pretends otherwise, which is itself honest.

## Two cross-cutting findings worth acting on

**Design is a wide-open gap.** 14 of 24 run WordPress, Squarespace, HubSpot or Divi/Avada
with default type. Not one site used a motion library beyond Swiper carousels; only three
had a deliberate font pairing. A genuinely crafted site would be visibly the best-looking
AI consultancy in UK/IE with modest effort.

**Pricing transparency is the strongest available differentiator and almost nobody uses it
well.** Only four sites publish numbers (theaiconsultancy.ai, hartzai.com, headofai.ai,
antonyslumbers.com). Meanwhile the sites with the most impressive logos — Marr, Grill,
Azhar — offer no path to buy anything. Pairing Acuity-grade quantified proof with The AI
Consultancy's published price table and Careful's free diagnostic would beat every site in
this list.
