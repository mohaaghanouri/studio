## Segment top 5

### 1. Risto Uuk — ristouuk.com (Estonia/Belgium)

The only site in the set with genuine typographic taste: Crimson Text at a generous measure
over a warm paper ground (`#f9f8f5`), Inter for UI, one photograph, no gradient, no glow,
no pill badges. The authority is unmatchable — he wrote into the EU AI Act's
general-purpose-AI provisions, runs a 50,000-subscriber newsletter on it, sits on Estonia's
PM-level AI advisory board, and has a Wiley book coming. What's instructive is the
restraint: the entire hero is one paragraph of plain biography and two buttons. The flaw is
the mirror image of the strength — no offer, no service, no price, so it converts on
reputation alone.

### 2. SmedjaAI (Oliver Brantin) — smedjaai.se (Sweden)

The most complete small-operator site here. Warm cream ground with burnt-orange display
type instead of the compulsory dark-mode-and-neon; his own photograph in a card with a live
status dot; and — the genuinely stealable part — **working AI agents embedded directly in
the page**, so a prospect tests the product before ever booking a call. Every price is in
the hero ("från 9 995 kr engångs + 995 kr/mån · inga dolda avgifter"), alongside F-skatt
approval, org-nr and GDPR. He is honest that he is one person: "ingen byrå, ingen säljare."
Thin on proof — two named clients, one testimonial — but the testimonial is specific
("8 hours a week on admin").

### 3. Antti Merilehto — merilehto.com (Finland)

Nordic restraint done properly: DM Serif Display for headings against a clean sans body,
generous white, no decoration doing work the copy should do. The proof is stacked but never
shouted — Speaker of the Year (Evento Awards), 800+ keynotes, two published books, a logo
wall, media marks from Yle, HS, MTV, Monocle and AP. The service architecture is the
lesson: exactly three offerings, numbered 01/02/03, each two lines long, plus an FAQ that
pre-answers the five real booking objections (length, language, industry fit) and a
same-day-reply promise.

### 4. Jarno Duursma — jarnoduursma.nl (Netherlands)

The benchmark for social-proof density in this whole sweep, worth studying purely as a
credibility machine: 18 named enterprise logos (Unilever, BMW, ING, Rabobank, PwC, DHL,
Nestlé, ABN AMRO), 1,500+ keynotes, a 9.1 average rating **broken out per named event**,
47,000 newsletter subscribers, a book, TEDx, and a press row spanning NOS, NRC, Volkskrant
and Nieuwsuur. Every element on the page is a trust signal and the same CTA repeats four
times. The design itself is a competent but unremarkable speaker-site layout — this one
wins on evidence architecture, not craft.

### 5. Satori — satoriml.se/ai-konsult (Sweden)

The most distinctive visual system in the set and the most commercially transparent. Built
in Framer, set entirely in JetBrains Mono on near-black with a single muted green — a
terminal aesthetic held with total consistency, which is a real bet (it costs readability
at long body lengths) but leaves no doubt you've been somewhere specific. Commercially it
does what almost nobody does: names its own method ("AI-Trappan", five steps you can stop
at), publishes every price in SEK, cites the market rate it's undercutting, and runs a
comparison table against Big Consulting, freelancers and in-house. Then it undermines all
of it — not one client, logo or testimonial anywhere on the page.

- **vanbeaumond.nl** (NL) — the only one publishing both hard case numbers *and* price
